# 異獸 AI 生圖提示詞

每隻異獸做成 **1024×1024、白底的 PNG**，在 App 的「美術工作室」拖進對應的格子就會套用。

## 讓畫風一致的做法
1. 每張圖都在前面貼上同一段「共用風格」。
2. 進化型請在同一個對話裡接著畫，並寫明「以上一張為基礎、明顯長大變強」，前後才會像同一隻。
3. 不要寫任何現有作品或角色的名字，只描述你要的樣子。

```
original creature design for a monster-collecting game, official character art in the style of 1990s handheld RPG concept sheets, single creature, full body, three-quarter view, chibi proportions with a big head and small round body, simple bold shapes, flat cel colors with only 3-4 colors and one shadow tone, uniform thick dark outline with slight hand-drawn wobble, minimal details, simple dot eyes with a small white shine highlight, no gradients, no glossy highlights, no glow, no rim light, no soft airbrush shading, no texture, plain white background, no text
```
